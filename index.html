How it works.

Pick a creator

Any X account. We read followers, name and avatar live — nothing is typed in by hand.

Launch on Pons

Bonding curve → Uniswap v4, liquidity locked by the PONS hook. The vault is the fee recipient.

Register a post

One X post per coin. Its view count is read from X every time the page loads.

Get paid

Every Sunday the vault pays the holders of the most-watched creator. Pro rata, on chain, in USDG.

  1. I

    One coin per creator. The first launch on a handle is the coin; later ones are not listed.

  2. II

    Views are read from X, never entered. A post that is deleted scores zero.

  3. III

    The vault only holds fee income. It never buys, never sells, never holds coins.

  4. IV

    The creator can claim their share any time by replying with their claim code. Until then it accrues to the vault.

Questions, answered.

Does the creator have to agree?

No. It's a coin about attention on a public account. The creator can claim the fee share any time, block nothing, and profit from being watched.

What counts as "views this week"?

The view count X shows on the registered post, read through our /api/tweet reader every time the page loads. One post per coin, changeable once a week by the coin's launcher.

Where does the money actually come from?

Only from the 1% trade fee on Pons. 70% is the creator side, which is the views vault here. No emissions, no treasury, no promises.

Who gets paid, exactly?

Every Sunday 00:00 UTC, the holders of the creator coin whose registered post has the most views that week — pro rata by balance at the snapshot, in USDG.

Can I launch on any account?

Any handle that exists on X. We read it live before you go to Pons; the first launch on a handle is that creator's coin, later ones are not listed.

Is the vault live?

Not yet. The vault address is published in docs the moment it's on chain, with the payout tx list under it.

Is the platform coin live?

$VIEWS launches on Pons; the CA is posted on X first and appears here the same minute.
